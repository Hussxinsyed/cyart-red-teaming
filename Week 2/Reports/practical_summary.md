# Practical Task Summary

During this week I performed practical cybersecurity tasks in a controlled lab environment.

I used Nmap to scan the target machine and identify open ports and services.

After identifying vulnerable services, I used Metasploit to perform exploitation testing.

I also used Wireshark to monitor and analyze network traffic including TCP, DNS and TLS communication.

These practical activities helped me understand scanning, exploitation and network analysis in a better way.

Skills learned:

- Service Enumeration
- Vulnerability Identification
- Exploitation Basics
- Traffic Analysis
- Documentation
