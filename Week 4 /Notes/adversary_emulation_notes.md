# Adversary Emulation Notes

Adversary emulation involves simulating the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) of known threat actors.

Frameworks such as MITRE Caldera can automate attack simulations in lab environments.

APT29 is commonly studied for phishing, persistence, and stealth techniques.

MITRE ATT&CK Navigator helps visualize attacker techniques and defensive coverage.

Adversary emulation improves blue team detection and incident response readiness.
