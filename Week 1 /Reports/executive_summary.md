# Executive Summary

During Week 1, I learned the fundamentals of Red Teaming and completed introductory security assessment activities in a controlled lab environment.

The practical exercises included network scanning with Nmap, studying vulnerability assessment concepts with Metasploit, and understanding post-exploitation techniques. These activities helped identify how attackers discover exposed services and potential weaknesses in systems.

The main lesson learned was the importance of proactive security testing, regular patching, and continuous monitoring. Organizations can reduce risk by minimizing exposed services, keeping systems updated, and implementing proper security controls.
