# Reconnaissance Notes

Reconnaissance is the first phase of an attack.

I learned the difference between passive and active reconnaissance.

Passive Recon:
- Search engines
- WHOIS lookup
- Public information gathering

Active Recon:
- Nmap scanning
- Service enumeration

During my lab practice, I used Nmap to identify open ports and running services on a target machine.

This helped me understand how attackers collect information before attempting exploitation.
