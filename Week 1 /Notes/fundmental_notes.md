# Red Team Fundamentals

This week I learned what Red Teaming is and how it helps organizations identify weaknesses before attackers can exploit them.

I understood the attack lifecycle:

1. Reconnaissance
2. Enumeration
3. Exploitation
4. Post Exploitation
5. Reporting

I also explored the MITRE ATT&CK framework and learned how attackers' actions are categorized into tactics and techniques.

The main takeaway from this week was understanding how ethical hackers simulate real-world attacks in a controlled environment.
